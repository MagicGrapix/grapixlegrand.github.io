Knockback Arena

2026–now · Personal prototype

Knockback Arena is a fast-paced multiplayer fighting game where every hit increases your knockback percentage. The higher your percentage gets, the farther you’ll be launched by the next attack.

Pick up melee weapons around the arena, use their limited charges wisely, or throw them directly at your opponents. Survive the chaos, send the other players flying off the map, and become the last one standing.

This gameplay prototype was designed and developed in Roblox Studio.
